New game announced: 7 Seals of Mephisto

October 26, 2026

Hey there! Today I'm excited to announce my upcoming game: 7 Seals of Mephisto!
It's a roguelite deckbuilder with combat inspired by modern, fast-paced TCG matches.

7 Seals of Mephisto

In 7 Seals of Mephisto you embrace the role of a summoner in a world the Dark Lord Mephisto has already conquered. Your journey leads through the remains of a shattered land, where each region is guarded by a terrible boss. Your army fits inside a deck of cards: deadly creatures, powerful spells, and legendary cards. Every run is an assault on Mephisto's dominion, and every match is a step toward the final duel where you either banish him back to the burning hells or are forgotten by a world already lost.

Want to know more? Head over to the 7 Seals of Mephisto Steam page for details, a trailer, and screenshots. And don't forget to wishlist.

ZpellCatz Update and Season 7

To celebrate the announcement of 7 Seals of Mephisto, I thought it's a good idea to also patch ZpellCatz. Version 1.9.0 brings more combat exp, more aoe for 'Fire Ball' and 'Shock Nova' and some item buffs.

Full Changelog (ZpellCatz)

  • Start: Already live!
  • End: November 15, 2026

The season theme "Gift of the Enchantress" returns for this season. Enjoy lots of enchantments and chaos enchantments! The following bonuses are active during the season:

  • All rare and epic items found are enchanted, chaos enchanted or both.
  • This bonus does not apply to crafted rare / epic items or ones gambled from the merchant.
  • However, enchantments and chaos enchantments stay when you upgrade or craft an item.
  • Treasure rooms and boss rooms drop an additional rare item.

As in the last seasons, the overall difficulty is lower due to the strong bonuses. Also, due to the chaotic nature of chaos enchantments, the power progression is all over the place. Nevertheless, it is interesting to stumble upon a wildly powerful item every now and then. Have fun!
